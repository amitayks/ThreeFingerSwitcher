## Why

The V2 conversational canvas (`ai-conversational-canvas`, archived unsynced) turned every AI-band fire into a typed multi-turn thread on the launcher canvas — and in practice that was the wrong home for it. A quick action (Fix Grammar, Translate) wants the v1 grammar back: **auto-fire → stream → two-finger DOWN applies / RIGHT discards — done**, no composer, no thread, nothing left behind. Meanwhile the conversation *does* deserve a durable home, and the notch dock (`ai-parked-sessions`, just shipped) already is one — but reaching it required parking a canvas conversation via overscroll, and pulling a card back dragged the session *out* of the notch and back onto the launcher canvas. This pivot separates the two worlds cleanly: **quick actions are ephemeral and never stored in the notch dock; conversations are born at the notch, operated at the notch, and die at the notch** (expiry or deletion). One surface per job, no bridge between them.

## What Changes

- **Revert the command band to v1 one-shot presets.** `AppCoordinator` routes AI-item fires (both the normal path and the screen-region path) back through `AICommandExecutor.fire(_:)`. The executor sheds its conversational half — `fireConversational`, `openConversationCanvas`, `send`/`continueConversation`, `extractLatest`, `parkHandoff`/`restoreConversation`, the approval-gate seams, composer attachments, and the `.awaitingSeed`/`.conversing`/`.awaitingTurn`/`.awaitingApproval`/`.parked` states. The canvas view drops the thread renderer, composer, float-up, and the field-focus key/main flip; the canonical resolve is again DOWN = commit (at top), RIGHT = discard, UP = scroll. **BREAKING** (vs. the unsynced V2 behavior only — the synced `ai-command-band` spec already describes this grammar; the delta adds an explicit "one-shot only, never a session" requirement so the boundary is spec-pinned).
- **Quick actions never touch the notch dock.** A one-shot fire creates no `AgentConversation`, is never handed to the park store/scheduler, and leaves no rail card — by construction (the executor no longer has an `onPark` seam). Overscroll-park (`OverscrollPark`, `canvasAtBottom`, `onParkCanvas`) is deleted.
- **Conversations become notch-native.** The multi-turn engine moves out of the executor into a `NotchSessionEngine` (MLX-free Core): the streaming turn loop, tool-routing/approval gate, thinking channel, and per-session state. Sessions are **opened from the notch** — the rail gains a persistent **"+ New chat"** card — and a card (new or existing) **expands in place** into a notch-anchored conversation panel: thread + typed composer (Enter sends; the panel flips key while the field is focused, on the `SwitcherPanel.keyInteractive` precedent, restoring focus on collapse), Approve/Skip buttons for a paused tool step, Copy on assistant answers. Collapse returns the session to the rail and background scheduling; the session **stays at the notch until expiry (the existing auto-dismiss countdown) or deletion (the existing authoritative discard)**. Restore-to-canvas is removed (`ParkController.onRestoreConversation`, `LauncherOverlayController.restoreCanvas`).
- **Everything else at the notch survives unchanged:** the store/scheduler/lifecycle (`ParkedSessionStore`, `SerialParkScheduler` one-active-now with the K-slot seam, eviction, needs-you escalation + ambient glow, terminal auto-dismiss), the merged-notch look (5a85e4b), and the cursor-reveal rail.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

_None — both surfaces already have capabilities; the pivot re-draws the boundary between them._

### Modified Capabilities

- `ai-command-band`: ADDED requirement pinning the one-shot grammar — a fired AI command auto-fires exactly one generation, resolves by DOWN-commit/RIGHT-discard, never opens a typed thread, never creates a durable session, and is never stored in the notch dock.
- `ai-parked-sessions`: the capability becomes **notch-native sessions** — REMOVED: overscroll-park (the canvas park trigger) and pull-back-restore-to-canvas; ADDED: open-from-the-notch (the "+ New chat" card), the in-place expanded conversation panel (thread, key-capable composer, approval buttons, copy), and collapse-back-to-background; MODIFIED: park-state/lifecycle requirements re-worded so sessions are born at the notch rather than handed off from the canvas (expiry + deletion semantics unchanged).

## Impact

- **Code (MLX-free Core, `swift test`):** `AI/AICommandExecutor.swift` (sheds the conversational half — its state enum returns to the v1 cases), new `AI/Parked/NotchSessionEngine.swift` (the re-homed turn loop + approval gate + tool steps), `AI/Parked/OverscrollPark.swift` deleted, `App/ParkController.swift` (drops `park(conversation)` handoff + `onRestoreConversation`; gains `newSession()` / `bind(id:)` / `collapse()` for the expanded panel), `AI/Agent/BareSeedDefault.swift` deleted (no seed at the notch).
- **Code (overlay/app target, `xcodebuild` compile-verify):** `Overlay/AICommandCanvasView.swift` (back to the one-shot preview: no thread/composer/float-up), `Overlay/LauncherOverlayController.swift` (drop `restoreCanvas`/`onParkCanvas`/the field-focus key flip), `Overlay/NotchHomeZoneOverlay.swift` + `Overlay/NotchHomeZoneController.swift` (the "+ New chat" card, the expanded conversation view, the key-flip + reveal-pinning while expanded), `App/AppCoordinator.swift` (rewire the two fire call sites; delete the park/restore/approval-compass wiring).
- **Tests:** `ConversationSessionTests` / `ConversationalCanvasTests` / the conversational halves of `AICommandExecutorTests` re-target `NotchSessionEngine`; `ParkedSessionsTests` drops overscroll-park/restore cases and gains new-session/expand/collapse/expire coverage; `BatchedRuntimeTests` re-targets the engine where it drove executor conversations.
- **Consumed, not redefined:** `AgentConversation`/`AgentMessage`/`AgentSessionID` (`ai-conversation-runtime`), the route loop + `ApprovalGate` (`ai-tool-routing`), the scheduler/store/lifecycle and reveal/anchor geometry (`ai-parked-sessions`, synced), `AIError.message(for:)`.
- **No new permission, no gesture relocation, no re-login.** The notch panel keeps the non-activating species; it becomes key only while the composer is focused (the canvas precedent), and teardown stays synchronous.
- **Out of scope:** composer attachments at the notch (image/screenshot staging — the engine keeps `send(_:images:)` so a later change can add the UI); any launcher→notch bridge (deliberately none); the batched K-slot runtime and media/fleet waves (untouched seams).
